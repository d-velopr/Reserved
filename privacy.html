Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Reserved LLC ("Reserved," "we," "us") collects through reservedwayornoway.com, what we do with it, and the choices you have.

  • We never sell your info
  • Analytics only if you say yes
  • Near me stays on your phone
  • Unsubscribe anytime
What we collect

What you give us. When you join our list at the entry gate or the Subscribe form, we collect your email address and, at the entry gate, your ZIP code. We also save the page you signed up from and the time.

Messages you send us. If you use the Contact page, we collect your name, email, your license number if you give one, and your message, and use them only to reply. Sending a message doesn't add you to our email list.

Analytics, only if you agree. When you click "Yes, enter" on the entry gate, you agree to analytics. We then use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to record things like the pages you visit, how long you stay, your device and browser type, how you found the site, and your approximate location (city level, based on your IP address). Analytics does not run before you agree, and we never turn on Google's advertising features.

Saved on your device. Your browser remembers that you've confirmed you're 21+, agreed to analytics and joined the list, so you don't see the gate on every visit. This stays on your device. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes it.

Your location, only in your browser. If you tap "Near me" on the Store Locator, your browser asks for your location. It's used on your device to sort the shops by distance and is never sent to us.

How we use it
  • To email you about new products, drops, restocks and shops near you.
  • To answer messages you send us.
  • To learn which areas want Reserved, so we know where to open next.
  • To see how the site is used and make it better.
  • To keep the site secure and meet our legal obligations.
Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising. We use a few service providers to run the site, and they handle information only to provide their service:

  • Google stores the sign-up list and contact messages (Google Sheets) and runs Google Analytics.
  • GitHub hosts the website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Esri provides the map tiles on the Store Locator. Zippopotam.us turns a ZIP code you search there into a map location.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and unpkg deliver the site's fonts and code, so your browser contacts them when a page loads.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

Links to Instagram, Google Maps and our retail shops take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Your choices
  • Unsubscribe with the link in any email from us, or contact us.
  • See, correct or delete the information we have about you: send us a request and we'll handle it within 45 days.
  • Turn off analytics by clearing this site's data in your browser, blocking cookies, or installing Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.
Do Not Track

Browsers' "Do Not Track" setting doesn't change how this site works. Analytics only runs if you agree at the entry gate.

California residents

California law gives you the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to not be treated differently for using these rights. Ask us through our Contact page. We don't share personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

21+ only

This site is for adults 21 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If we find out we have, we delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email and ZIP code until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or service is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions or requests: use our Contact page.
