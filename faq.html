FAQ

Quick answers about Reserved.

Where can I buy Reserved?

At licensed shops across Southern California. Open the Store Locator and search by city, shop name or ZIP code, or tap "Near me". Stock changes, so call the shop before you head over.

Can I order online or get delivery?

Not from this site. We don't sell or ship anything here. Some of our shops take online orders or deliver through their own websites, so check with the shop.

What do you make?

Flower (listed by strain), pre-rolls, concentrates, vapes and edibles. See everything in the Collection.

Is it lab tested?

Yes. California law requires every batch of legal cannabis to pass testing at a state-licensed lab before it can be sold. Potency is printed on the package.

How do I unsubscribe?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email from us, or send us a message and we'll take you off the list.

When are the new shops opening?

Upcoming shops are listed under Coming Soon on the Home page. You're already on the list, so you'll hear when they open.

How should I store my products?

Sealed, somewhere cool and dark, and out of reach of kids and pets.

I run a dispensary. How do I carry Reserved?

Send us a message with your shop name and city.
